Currently I'm interested in exploring what different sets of universal constraints can tell us about the full space of all CFTs. With the conformal bootstrap, I'm exploring how consistency of stress tensor correlators might give us a "world atlas" of 3D CFTs. With the entanglement bootstrap, I'm exploring how information-theoretic consistency conditions on density matrices in finite 1+1D quantum systems might give us a catalog of critical point ground states, and in turn 2D CFTs. In both cases, the hope is that we might find new, previously-undiscovered CFTs.
